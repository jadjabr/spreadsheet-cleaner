# Sendovia

Sendovia is a local growth partner for Edmonton businesses: it makes sure people find them, reach them, and come back. The brand is black and blue, confident and plain-spoken, built to look sharp on a phone screen.

## Voice

- Talk in outcomes, not tools: "every lead gets answered in seconds", not "n8n workflow".
- Short sentences, plain words, first person from Jad ("I make sure…").
- Never mention automation platforms, APIs or frameworks in client-facing copy.
- Core line: **"I make sure people find you, reach you, and come back."**

## Logo

- The icon: a blue (`brand-blue`) rounded square (`radius-md` at 62px) with a white curved arc rising from bottom-left to top-right, ending in a white dot, like a message being sent.
- The wordmark: lowercase **sendovia**, regular weight in the `logo` family; "send" in `ink-on-card`, "ovia" in `brand-blue`.
- Use `sendovia-logo-light.svg` on white or off-white (`card`); on black use `sendovia-logo-dark.svg` (white "send", light-blue "ovia"), or place the light version on a `card` panel, as the services sheet does.
- Give the logo clear space at least the height of the icon's dot on every side. Don't recolour, stretch or add effects.

## Colour

- Black `ground` is the signature background; `brand-blue` is the one accent. No other hues.
- On black, blue text uses `accent-text` (light blue) for contrast; solid blue fills (`brand-blue`) carry white text.
- Off-white `card` panels on black hold the detailed content; text inside them uses `ink-on-card` and `ink-on-card-muted`.
- Service tags: `tag-bg` fill with `tag-ink` text, `radius-pill`.

## Type

- **DM Sans** (Google Fonts) for everything: headings at 700, body at 400.
- The wordmark alone uses the Helvetica-style `logo` family.
- Labels (`label` style) are uppercase with about 0.1em letter spacing, in `accent-text`.

## Layout

- Mobile first: 24px side margins (`space-6`), 32px between sections (`space-8`).
- Cards: `radius-lg`, `space-5` padding, numbered with a 32px `brand-blue` badge (`radius-sm`).
- End pages with a `brand-blue` call-to-action band.
- No gradients, shadows, emoji or left-border cards.
